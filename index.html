The Fest

Built on IIT Bombay's culture of building things that matter.

Every winter, the Powai campus turns into a live testing ground for robotics arenas, hack rooms, and open-air stages. Thousands of students, makers and researchers arrive to compete, present and trade ideas over 72 hours.

It's run entirely by an undergraduate student team — from arena wiring to stage lighting — with mentorship from faculty and alumni across the institute.

  • ✓ 80+ competitions across robotics, coding, design & core sciences
  • ✓ Keynotes and workshops from researchers and founders
  • ✓ Open-campus exhibits, night markets and a closing concert
